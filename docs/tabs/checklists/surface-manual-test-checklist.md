# Surface manual test checklist

See the [feature guide](../../modeling/surface-and-nurbs/README.md). Record the Prism build and any result that differs from the expected behavior.

- [ ] Extrude an open line into a surface. Confirm it remains a surface and measure the extrusion depth.
- [ ] Trim an overlapping surface and inspect the remaining boundary. Undo, then sew two surfaces that share an edge and check the seam.
- [ ] Thicken a simple planar surface by a known amount. Measure the resulting solid thickness.

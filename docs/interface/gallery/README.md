# Startup model gallery

## Status and scope

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-05 after the on-demand GitHub gallery proposal: use existing Prism studies and Claude's camera lens, represent Solid, Surface/NURBS and Freeform, and add three new finished examples to exercise modeling, Appearance and API/MCP. [Delivery issue #61](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/61).

## Behavior

- Home shows **Made with Prism** with image cards, workflow/category, description, and a credit line noting the example was made with Prism MCP alongside its author and license. The section text links the examples to the same MCP toolkit configurable in Settings → AI Assistant. Search matches names, descriptions and workflow. Existing workspace, recent files and new/open actions remain available.
- Cards are grouped into titled sections by the first workflow in a model's Workflow field, in the order of the workspace tabs: Solid, Surface, Freeform, NURBS, Sheet Metal, CAM, CAE, ECAD (a model with another first workflow gets its own section after these; empty sections are hidden). Each section shows its first three models, in catalog order, with **Show all N** / **Show fewer**; the expanded state lasts while Home is open. Searching shows every match, grouped, without the limit. *Owner direction 2026-11-08 ([#84](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/84)); the section order and the three-model limit are the owner's initial choice and may change as the collection grows.* Tests: GAL-U007, GAL-H003.
- A model that has **extra files** (an animation video, an exploded view image) shows its action as a split button: the main button still downloads and opens the model, and its dropdown offers *Download animation video (MP4)* or *Download exploded view (PNG)*. Extras are listed in the catalog entry's optional `Extras` (label, file name, URL, size, SHA-256), hosted in `gallery/extras/` on GitHub like models, verified on download, and saved to the workspace's `Gallery` folder under a new name, never overwriting a file. The status line names the saved path. *Owner request 2026-11-08 ([#84](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/84)).* Tests: GAL-U008. The Meridian orrery offers its drive animation and an exploded view.
- Only the small catalog ships with Prism. Models and previews live in the public repository's `gallery/` directory, outside application assets and packaging. Small initial files use GitHub raw downloads; the catalog also supports GitHub Release asset URLs for larger collections later.
- Home loads the local catalog immediately and refreshes the remote catalog/previews asynchronously. Failure never blocks startup; Refresh retries. Last valid catalog and previews remain available offline. A first offline launch shows catalog text and preview placeholders.
- A card downloads its model only on request, checks size and SHA-256, and atomically caches the verified file. Cached models work offline. A newer content hash gets a separate cache entry.
- Opening always creates a uniquely named editable copy under the workspace's `Gallery` folder (or Documents/Prism/Gallery when no workspace is selected). Cache files and previous working copies are never overwritten. Saving edits cannot alter the downloadable original.
- Catalog entries include stable ID, workflow, author, license, minimum Prism version, HTTPS model/preview URLs, model size and SHA-256. Unsupported newer minimum versions cannot open. Catalog schema errors retain the prior catalog. Downloads are bounded and time out; partial/corrupt downloads are not offered as cached models.
- Curated examples are concept studies. Geometry and appearance are retained in native files; older studies may lack construction history. The Surface pendant retains its hidden meridian and source surface; the Freeform chair retains editable cages. Reproducible scripts are linked from the content README.

## Acceptance and maintenance

Startup stays responsive without a network connection. Models do not enter the installer. Verified cache opens without network; repeated opens create independent copies. Downloads with changed/truncated bytes fail clearly. The collection represents multiple workflows and can replace weaker examples by updating the catalog independently of application releases.

## Curated collection

**Open-end wrench** (workflow `CAE / Solid`, listed under CAE; [#85](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/85)) is a 6 mm flat steel wrench for 13 mm nuts with a static structural study: jaw flats fixed, 200 N sideways at the handle end, 2 mm 10-node tetrahedra (12,795 elements). Max von Mises 151 MPa, tip displacement 0.695 mm, safety factor 1.65 against A36 yield (250 MPa), reactions balance the load. Beam theory for the handle gives 0.784 mm and 117 MPa at the root; the peak is higher because it sits at the neck and jaw support. A 3 mm mesh agrees on displacement within 5%. The saved model has the body only because study results are not stored in the file. [Source script](../../../gallery/build_wrench.py), [render](evidence/wrench-render.png). An illustration, not a certified tool design.

Owner asked on 2026-11-08 ([#84](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/84)) to try CNC CAM on a wooden product. **Maple serving board** (workflow `CAM / Solid`, so it lists under CAM) is a 300 × 160 × 18 mm board with R25 corners, a 4 mm deep juice well and a through hand slot, with a saved CAM setup (6 mm wood end mill, 3 mm stock allowance, G54 at the stock's lower-left top corner) and four toolpaths: face, pocket the well, pocket the slot through, tabbed profile. About 17 minutes of machining; stock review finds no gouges, rapid contacts or holder contacts. Planning it exposed three CAM bugs fixed in the same change (button parameters through MCP, Post's folder path, a false rapid contact on spoilboard retracts). [Source script](../../../gallery/build_serving_board.py), [GAL-I009](../../../Prism.Tests/Integration/DesktopGalleryTests.cs) (written, not run on this Linux workstation), [render](evidence/serving-board-render.png). Decorative: feeds, speeds and tabs are placeholders, so prove out any program on scrap.

Owner asked on 2026-11-08 ([#83](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/83)) for another sheet-metal part that mostly just looks good, with holes and trapezoid sides. **Lotus dish** is a silver dish folded with the Fold tool from one flat sketch: a 70 mm square base with four petals, each a rectangular stretch that rises 50° followed by a trapezoid tip (70 mm narrowing to 30 mm over 45 mm) that curls back outward 30°, so eight bends. A centre hole and a ring of eight holes pierce the base, each petal has a slot and each tip a round hole. It is 1.5 mm brushed aluminium with a 2 mm inside radius. [Source script](../../../gallery/build_lotus_dish.py).

A bend zone must be a clean rectangle of metal, so the petals are rectangles through both bends and the trapezoids sit beyond the second one; slanted edges crossing a zone would make Fold refuse the bend. The script derives the signed angle of each bend in Fold's own order (left to right, then bottom to top) and checks that the folded volume equals t × (flat area − bend zones) + the bend sectors, and that the flat pattern keeps all 17 holes. Building it exposed a Fold bug, fixed in the same change: a bend line was not truly infinite, because its cutting strip was centered on the line's first drawn point, so lines drawn far from the sheet cut only part of it and Fold said the bends did not divide the outline (FLD-H014). [GAL-I008](../../../Prism.Tests/Integration/DesktopGalleryTests.cs) checks the volume and hole count against the flat pattern. Reviewed actual Prism renders: [hero](evidence/lotus-dish-render.png) and [dish beside its flat pattern](evidence/lotus-dish-flat.png).

Owner asked on 2026-11-08 ([#82](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/82)) for sheet-metal demo parts to be plain silver, for a cardboard box built with the sheet-metal tools in a brown box-material color, and for the growing gallery to be organized. **Cardboard box** is a shoebox-style carton with a hinged lid, a front lip and a handle slot in each short wall, folded in one step from a single plus-shaped die-line sketch with the **Fold** tool and shown beside its own flat die line. [Source script](../../../gallery/build_cardboard_box.py).

It is 3 mm corrugated board (library material *Corrugated Cardboard*, kraft brown, with a slight bump) with a 0.5 mm inside radius as the score and K = 0.33. The inside is 150 × 100 × 60 mm; the box measures 157 × 111 × 67 mm with the lip, and the die line is 274.7 × 358.4 mm with an area of 65,519 mm² after the two 9 × 32 mm slots. The lid is slightly larger than the base, so the model uses Fold's **Fixed region** set to the region over the sketch origin; with the default (the largest region) the box would come out upside down. The model has four objects: the box, the die line, its fold lines and the hidden sketch. The Fold tablet stand is now brushed silver instead of painted, since sheet metal is expected to look like metal; the gallery index in [gallery/README.md](../../../gallery/README.md) groups the models by workflow and the catalog check requires every model to be listed there.

Building it showed two gaps in Fold, both fixed in the same change: Fold fused flanges that overlap each other without a message (it now refuses, naming the overlap volume; FLD-H012) and it always held the largest region fixed (it now has the Fixed region choice; FLD-H013). [GAL-I007](../../../Prism.Tests/Integration/DesktopGalleryTests.cs) checks the box is one valid solid of 157 × 111 × 67 mm, that its die line has the formula's area and both slots, and captures two views. Reviewed actual Prism renders: [front quarter](evidence/cardboard-box-render.png) and [rear with the lid](evidence/cardboard-box-lid.png).

Owner requested a sheet-metal study on 2026-11-08 ([#80](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/80)): not overly complicated, but showing more than the absolute basics. **Fold** (the tablet stand, not to be confused with the Fold tool added to Sheet Metal on the same day) is a one-piece tablet stand in the Sheet Metal workflow. One Flange profile gives three bends, two of them at 110° rather than 90°, and a closed hem along the top edge. A 30-hole round vent pattern in the leaning back rest, a cable hole and chamfered corners are cut through the bent body, and Flat Pattern unfolds the finished part beside it with every hole. A placeholder tablet shows scale. [Source script](../../../gallery/build_sheetmetal.py).

The 2 mm brushed silver aluminium sheet has a 3 mm inside bend radius and a 140 mm width; the unfolded blank is 206.18 × 140 mm at K = 0.44, which hand calculation from the bend allowance reproduces to four decimals (206.1838). Fold has seven objects: the solid, the unfolded surface and its bend lines, the tablet and three hidden source sketches. Corners are chamfered, not rounded, and the holes are round, because Flat Pattern currently rejects slots and rounded corners ([#78](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/78)). A cut from a face sketch on the tilted wall also removed nothing in its default direction ([#79](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/79)), so the script checks the removed volume against hole area × thickness and repeats the cut reversed when needed. The bend lines are curves in the document and did not show in the path-traced renders.

[GAL-I006](../../../Prism.Tests/Integration/DesktopGalleryTests.cs) checks that all 30 vent walls and the cable hole are 2 mm deep (a zero-depth cut leaves degenerate discs), that the flat pattern keeps all 31 holes and is 140 mm wide, the 206.18 mm development against an independent bend-allowance calculation (straight legs plus θ·(r + K·t) per bend, hem as a 180° bend of radius gap/2), and that the tablet clears the stand. GAL-I001 covers shared validity and saved appearance on reopen. Reviewed actual Prism renders: [hero](evidence/fold-stand-render.png), [unfolded beside the part](evidence/fold-stand-unfolded.png) and [bends and hem](evidence/fold-stand-bends.png).

Owner requested another showcase model on 2026-11-07 ([#74](https://github.com/chrizpro/prism/issues/74)): something that amazes people, may use assembly, need not be quick, built mostly in the Solid workflow with good appearances, and doubling as a check that recent work did not break modeling. **Meridian** is a geared brass orrery and the first gallery assembly. A walnut plinth carries a brass movement: six nested orbit tubes on a stepped fixed arbor, a blued-steel layshaft with five involute wheels and an ebony winding knob, and a two-stage idler that drives Saturn from Jupiter's tube. Arms carry textured planets and Saturn's rings around a frosted-glass sun lit from inside, above an enamel zodiac ring. [Source script](../../../gallery/build_orrery.py), [planet maps](../../../gallery/orrery_textures.py).

Meridian has 30 exact solids (19 assembly parts, the rest static plinth, ring and sun bodies), 9 revolute and 9 rigid joints, 8 gear couplings that use the modeled tooth counts, one lamp and 45 hidden source sketches. Gear profiles are generated by the script, so their sketches are not kept. **Assemble › Drive Joints** on *Revolute 1* (the knob) turns Mercury 4, Venus 62/38, Earth 1, Mars 35/65, Jupiter 12/138 and Saturn 12/138 × 42/84 × 56/70 times per knob turn, all in one direction; the Moon follows Earth's arm at 37:3. Wheels are phased so every mesh seats a tooth in a gap, with 0.24 mm backlash. The ratios approximate real periods (Mercury 0.250 vs 0.241 years, Saturn 28.75 vs 29.46 years); couplings are ideal, without tooth contact or friction, and sizes and distances are not to scale. The model is 6.5 MB, larger than the other studies because of about 900 B-rep gear teeth. During review the idler was moved to hang from the top plate after the interference check showed Jupiter's 138-tooth wheel sweeping through its original arbor.

[GAL-I005](../../../Prism.Tests/Integration/DesktopGalleryTests.cs) drives the knob 36° and checks each planet's turn against its tooth ratio and the Moon's 37:3 follow, checks that all planets share one orbital plane, runs Interference on the layshaft, idler and carrier meshes, and captures three views. GAL-I001 covers shared geometry validity and saved appearance on reopen. Reviewed actual Prism renders: [hero](evidence/meridian-render.png), [movement close-up](evidence/meridian-movement.png), [top](evidence/meridian-top.png), [side](evidence/meridian-side.png) and [one knob turn](evidence/meridian-orbit.gif) (48 frames, Drive Joints per frame).

## Open decisions and limits

Community submissions, ratings, accounts and assembly insertion from online cards are future proposals. No automatic replacement of a user's working copy. GitHub availability is needed for uncached downloads; a separate library repository or release assets can be adopted as the collection grows.

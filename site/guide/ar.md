# AR and sharing

Use **Share → AR & sharing → AR Export** from Home, Assemble, Render, or Animate. AR is a sharing tool within those workflows.

## Export a model

1. Give the export a name.
2. Choose real size, tabletop scale, or fit to one metre.
3. Choose **Export for AR** to create the files, or **Export and share to phone** to serve a local viewing page.
4. Open the displayed address on a phone on the same Wi-Fi network.

Prism exports GLB for compatible Android and web viewers and USDZ for supported Apple viewers. Actual AR availability depends on the device and its viewer.

## Stop sharing

Choose **Stop Sharing** in the same ribbon menu or **Stop sharing** in the AR tool panel. The ribbon action is enabled while the local sharing server is running.

Exported packages live under `Documents/Prism/AR` by default. Local sharing is intended for nearby devices on your network; it does not publish your model to a hosted website.
